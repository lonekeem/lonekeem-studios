# After the Quiet — Story Mode (v1.3.0) · Mature 18+
Lone Keem Studios presents. Riverside, Maine. One night. Text-only dialogue.

**Cast:** Keem (lead, a Black boy with a backpack) · Dad (Marcus) · Nia (little sister) and Biscuit the rabbit · Mr. Walt and Mr. Dube (neighbors) · Ruth (deputy) · Big Gus (the barber, mini-boss).

0. **The House** — Screams wake Keem in his boarded-up house. Through the gaps in the boards he watches Mr. Dube tackled and Mr. Walt being eaten. Gear up: backpack, bat, Dad's lockbox pistol (with a very Dad note). Pry the door.
1. **The Street** — Fight through the yard and the first runners toward the Pell house.
2. **The Pell House** — Dad, Nia and Biscuit. Banter. Get the patrol rifle from Ruth's cruiser while she is being eaten.
3. **Hank's Garage** — A horde. Shoot the fuel tanker. Big boom.
4. **The Truck** — Drive, ram zombies, crash near the bridge.
5. **Big Gus** — Mini-boss: ground slams and charges. 900 HP.
6. **The Bridge** — The family crosses. Dad stays behind to hold the gate: "Right behind you."

Collectibles: 8 *Captain Riverside* comic pages. Death = bite/feast animation, red YOU DIED, retry from the chapter checkpoint.

## Chapter 2 · High Water (v1.5.0)
Minutes after the bridge. Keem and Nia reach Haverford, the next town south. The radio calls it a "safe zone." It isn't one.
- **The Checkpoint:** the National Guard post is overrun, and the soldiers are being eaten. In the command tent, a dying Sgt. Okafor's radio message says the last evac helicopter leaves Haverford High stadium at 0600. The gate runs on a backup panel that needs fuses. Grabbing the Guard's supplies unlocks the **Guard SAW** in the Armory.
- **High Water (free roam):** the town is flooded. Find 3 fuses at Haverford Hardware, the Doughnut Depot, and the Suds-n-Duds laundromat (spin-cycle massacre included). There is loot to grab and Captain Riverside pages #9–#14 to find.
- **Hold the Gate (horde):** survive 60 seconds of waves while Nia wires the panel. Dad shows up, "right behind you," two hours late with a doughnut, and starts blasting.
- **Boss: Coach Brickman:** the gym teacher who failed Keem, now a 1.75× linebacker zombie.
- **Cliffhanger:** the scoreboard says the evac left early and to go to Portland. Dad's sleeve is wet with blood. "It's nothing, baby. Glass. From the gate."
New voices (Kokoro stock): Sgt. Okafor (am_eric, radio filter) and Coach Brickman (am_onyx).


## Chapter 3 — Route 1
On the road to Portland with a near-empty tank. The family clears the Big Moose Truck Stop for diesel, and at the Pine Tree Motel Keem finds the bite on Dad's arm from the Haverford gate. Dad: "Don't tell your sister. Not yet." Boss: Big Rig Ronnie. New weapon: chainsaw. The Tollmen announce over the radio that they own the turnpike.

## Chapter 4 — Everybody Pays
At the Maine Turnpike toll plaza the Tollmen raiders (human enemies that strafe and shoot) ambush the family. Deacon takes Dad and Nia because "the Captain pays double for biters." Keem storms the camp, breaks the cage and kills Deacon. New weapon: lever-action .30-30. Dad loses feeling in his hand.

## Chapter 5 — Black Friday
The Maine Mall. New survivors: Dr. Imani Reyes, a veterinarian, and Tuck, a kiosk kid. Keem finds three antiviral packs while the others hold the doors so Reyes can dose Dad; it buys him days. Boss: Mall Santa. New weapon: Auto-12 shotgun. Reyes has heard a "Navy" broadcast about a cure trial at Fort Williams that wants infected volunteers.

## Chapter 6 — The Lighthouse
Fort Williams and Portland Head Light. The family survives a Tollmen raid and kills the Toll King, whose last words are "The Captain… he doesn't cure them…". They signal the ship, and the "Captain" radios back: bring the infected one to the shore, alone. Dad's eyes go gray: "Keem… run." One gunshot, off-screen. **To be continued — Chapter 7 coming soon.**

## Chapter 12 — Sublevel B
Under Mass General, Vance's intercom guides the family through the morgue (sleeping Authority-infected, stay quiet or crouch), the boiler room (three fuses, each one wakes a wave), then power-on opens the sound-lock to the research hall. Hold the hall for 45 seconds while the glass cells crack open; Nia sits at the hatch of cell Subject 02 and talks to Mom through it. Boss: the Night Orderly, a mop-wielding zombie ("Visiting hours are over"). Reunion: Dana (Mom) is alive but turning, with gray-eyed moments; Keem holds her wrists, Dad sits down because his legs gave out, Biscuit pops out of Nia's hoodie. The alarm sounds with the power off. "Hi, baby."
New cast: Dana Pell (Mom), The Night Orderly.

## Chapter 13 — Biscuit Goes Rogue
Biscuit bolts the moment someone says "soup". Chase him up through the pack room, gift shop (a runaway gurney gag), the children's ward (two locked room fights, kill-count clears) and into the cafeteria. Boss: Lunch Lady Doreen ("Lunch is served." / "Seconds?"), slow soup puddles, ladle slams. Finale: Biscuit is sitting on the soup kettle; Keem picks him up. Vance: "…the alarm was not false."
New cast: Lunch Lady Doreen.

## Chapter 14 — Every Bite
Minutes after the cafeteria. The alarm is real. The family brings Biscuit to Vance's Level 9 research lab, where Reyes is already waiting. Vance runs three samples — Keem, Mom, and Biscuit — and the analyzer plays back every bite Keem has taken tonight. The first result stops the room: Keem is immune. Mom confesses she has always known, because she carried the virus without turning and it passed to him. That is why she was at Mass General, and why Subject One failed.

Then a second result lands. Biscuit has the same marker. Vance pulls a year-one animal-trial file: Subject R-07, "failed to turn," signed out of the building by D. Pell. Mom brought the rabbit home when Nia was four and told them he was from the shop on Elm. The soup chase suddenly has a second meaning: Biscuit knew these halls. Vance wanted the rabbit because he is the first proof the immunity can live in something that is not a Pell.

The Harbor Authority storms the lab for "the boy and the animal." Vance, who once said "I could. I won't," comes down and fights with a tranq rifle. Boss: the Warden, a riot-armored trooper who wants them alive — containment snares, and a grab for Biscuit. Vance stays behind to burn the samples and holds the lift. "Every single bite. Do not waste them."
New cast: The Warden. Captain Riverside #23: "The Boy Who Wouldn't Turn".

## Chapter 15 — PFC Danny Is So Sorry
The lift lets the family out on the Haverford waterfront. Tuck is back on the walkie: the searchlights split them up at Quarantine Row, and he is hiding in the Harborside Market freezer with a pilot. The pilot is PFC Danny, the evac soldier who left the stadium early, now living in a lobster costume and writing apology letters ("Dear Haverford, I'm so sorry"). He rigged the market with trap plates and hanging fish crates (squeeze a rubber chicken to lure zombies onto them), and he hid his helicopter's three parts where "nobody could steal them": the key in the fish counter, the battery in the cheese cave, and the fuse in the bakery oven. Find the parts, pull the levers, and hold for 45 seconds while everything goes up to the roof. Boss: Larry the Lobster, the market's old mascot, who turned inside his suit. He charges; dodge so he crashes into a stall, then hit him while he is stuck.
New cast: PFC Danny, Larry the Lobster. Captain Riverside #24: "Lobster Men Return".

## Chapter 16 — Last Light Out
On the market roof, Danny runs his pre-flight check ("Fuel: some. Rotor: yes. Courage: working on it."). Dr. Reyes catches up with Vance's last cooler of Keem's blood: "Every single bite." Commander Harlow of the Harbor Authority comes on the radio: one stairwell, think about that. Hold the roof for 60 seconds while the rotor spins up — waves come up the stairs and both ladders, and Mom can smell them first. Boss: Commander Harlow, front riot shield and flashbangs. At 40%, one of the Authority's own caged test subjects gets loose and goes for him. Everyone boards, the helicopter lifts off under tracer fire, and on the way north the radio crackles: Mr. Dube, in his basement on Keem's street. "It's still quiet here." Keem: "Then take us home." Card shows "To be continued — Chapter 17 coming soon." (v1.9.66; from v1.9.67 the story continues straight into Chapter 17.)
New cast: Commander Harlow. Captain Riverside #25: "Last Chopper Out of Town".

## Chapter 17 — Homecoming
Danny sets the helicopter down in the Riverside Market lot ("Riverside, Maine. Told you. Barely."); he and Tuck stay with the bird. Keem, Dad, Mom, Nia and Dr. Reyes walk Elm Street, past Mrs. Ortiz's crooked car, clearing yards and porches. Boss: Mr. Walt, the neighbor who gave out full-size candy bars every Halloween — turned, lunging, with runners pouring over the fences at half health. "Rest easy, Mr. Walt." In Mr. Dube's cellar, Dube is alive: Walt took the bite meant for him. He hands Dad back the hammer he left on the porch in March. On the radio, the Director of the Harbor Authority announces the Quiet Protocol: at dawn every uncleared town north of the river burns. Reyes: a cure is a reason not to — she needs a centrifuge, a fridge and power. Dube knows the place: Doc Ames' animal clinic on Mill Road. Hold the porch 45 seconds against Authority scouts while Dube works his shotgun. The Director cuts in: "Keem. Welcome home." Keem: "Cure first. Then the radio."
Captain Riverside #26: "There's No Place Like Home".

## Chapter 18 — Right Behind You
Dube drives them down Mill Road to Riverside Animal Clinic, where Deputy Ruth's cruiser still has its lights going. Clear the lot, then go in dark: zombies stand still in the kennels until you get close. Find the fuel can and get the generator running. In surgery, Dr. Reyes draws Keem's blood ("Little pinch. Every single bite, remember?") — but Dad's hand has started shaking since Chapter 14, and now the edges are going gray. Mom: "Marcus, look at me." Hold the clinic for 60 seconds while the centrifuge spins, with waves from the front and both back doors. Boss: Deputy Ruth, still in her campaign hat, firing yellow taser rings, with her siren calling more. "Rest now, Ruth. You did your shift." One dose. Dad stirs: "…Keem?" — "I can feel my hand." The Director wants the cure; Reyes: then nobody needs the Authority to decide who gets saved. Dube: put the recipe on the air from Signal Hill. Card shows "To be continued — Chapter 19 coming soon."
New cast: the Director, Mr. Dube (in person), Deputy Ruth. Captain Riverside #27: "The Vet Will See You Now".
